Quick Start

Upload many records at once from an Excel sheet.

  1. Download the predefined Excel template with the here link in the About this page box, and fill in your data.
  2. Click Upload AsphaltMine Excel File and select your completed file.
  3. Under Sheet, choose Database (Columns) or Database (Rows), whichever matches your file.
  4. Click Extract, then Confirm.
  5. When the extraction is successful, click Validate, then Confirm.
  6. If all records are valid, choose a workspace if you want one, or keep the records private.
  7. Click Save, then Confirm.

Good to know

  • One row with results for more than one test creates one record per test.
  • If some records are invalid, correct your Excel file and upload it again.
  • Saving needs write access, which you can request from My Profile.
