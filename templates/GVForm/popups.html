Quick Start

Enter a new test record in five steps.

  1. Click the box under Performance Tests on the right, tick the test or tests you want, then click Apply.
  2. Open each section with its Show tab: Data Source, Mixture, Results and Notes.
  3. Fill in the required fields, marked with a red asterisk. Click the information icon next to a field label for an explanation and an example.
  4. Click Save at the bottom of the form.
  5. In the confirmation box, keep the record private or assign it to a workspace, then click Confirm.

Good to know

  • Select several tests to enter them together. One record is saved per test.
  • To work from an existing record, use the Upload Form at the top, choose a record, tick the parts you want and click Load.
  • Click Download to save a record to your device as an XML file.
  • Saving needs write access, which you can request from My Profile.
